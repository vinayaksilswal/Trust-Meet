# Trust and safety

The initial build gates conversations behind a verified email, active account and local adult eligibility. Users expressly accept an invitation before media access. The app starts with the microphone and camera off. Either participant can end a call, block the other participant, or submit a categorized report. A block prevents another invitation with the blocked person. Reports persist for future review.

Staffed triage, case assignment, escalation, appeals, privileged suspension controls, audit trails and evidence policy are pending. Local reports go to a PostgreSQL table. No staffed reporting or emergency service currently operates.
