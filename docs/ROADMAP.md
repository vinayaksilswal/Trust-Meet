# Conviya delivery roadmap

1. Foundation — Next.js web shell, FastAPI domain boundaries, schema and migrations, PostgreSQL sessions, Redis throttling, email verification and development age assurance.
2. Real conversations — authenticated expiring invitations, explicit consent, room-scoped LiveKit grants, text, microphone and video, two-browser verification.
3. Secure matchmaking — Redis availability and matching, PostgreSQL conflict prevention, invitation timeouts, filters, blocking and skip.
4. Private rooms — scheduled group rooms, host admission controls and scoped invitations.
5. TrustOS — moderation console, suspensions, appeals, escalation and evidence governance.
6. Business Connect — professional profiles, mentor discovery and topic communities.
7. Monetization — entitlements, appointments, Razorpay test checkout, verified webhooks and ledger.
8. Advanced verticals — gated wellness, fitness and astrology capabilities.
9. Enterprise hardening — security, privacy, resilience, observability, backups and disaster recovery.
10. Controlled launch — legal, safeguarding, media, operational and commercial approval before public access.

See [Test report](TEST_REPORT.md) for executed checks and [Production readiness](PRODUCTION_READINESS.md) for open gates.
