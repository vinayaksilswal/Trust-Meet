# Product requirements

Trust Meet serves adults aged 18 and older, initially in India. Discovery presents six experiences. The development release enables Random Connect by invitation or a live availability queue. Users sign in with an email address and password, and enter their date of birth before joining a conversation. Participants can choose text, voice or video, withdraw at any time and report or block another participant.

Private Rooms supports hosts, expiring links, optional passwords, waiting-room approval, scheduling, capacity and group LiveKit calls. Professional networking, wellness, fitness and astrology are closed. Public access and paid services require the release gates documented in the [production readiness checklist](PRODUCTION_READINESS.md).
